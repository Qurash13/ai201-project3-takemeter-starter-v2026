# Copied from the README so the baseline is judged on exactly these
# definitions. baseline.py reads this file.

evidenced: makes a point and supports it with at least one specific a reader could check without asking the commenter: a number, date, link, named source, named product/version/event, or a concrete technical fact
opinion: states a position, judgment or prediction in earnest, with no checkable specific offered in support, however long or confident it is
quip: a joke, sarcastic jab, or bare reaction (thanks, congrats, agreement, eye-roll) that argues no position
question: a comment whose main purpose is to ask for information or clarification the commenter does not already have
